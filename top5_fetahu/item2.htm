FUN FACTS

  • Fortnite's Battle Royale mode was thrown together in just two months using assets from a failing, six-year-old zombie-survival project.
  • The iconic banana skin Peely was actually created by Epic Games' former chief creative officer specifically to troll his brother, who absolutely loves Fortnite but completely hates bananas.
  • To promote a 2018 in-game event, Epic Games physically placed the map's giant, missing Durrr Burger statue in the middle of a real-world California desert, sparking a massive real-life mystery investigation among fans.
