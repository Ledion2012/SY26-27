FUN FACTS

  • The iconic "Nuketown" map was inspired by an actual 1950s US government nuclear test site in Nevada, complete with creepy mannequins used to study radiation effects.
  • The original pitch for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare was initially rejected by Activision executives, who mistakenly believed that modern military shooters were a dead genre that players wouldn't buy.
  • Call of Duty: Ghosts actually cast a real-life Navy SEAL dog handler to provide motion-capture data for the game’s beloved German Shepherd, Riley.
