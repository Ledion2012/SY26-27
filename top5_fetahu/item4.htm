FUN FACTS

  • It was originally called Dynablocks (and briefly DinoBlocks) during its alpha and beta testing phases between 2004 and 2005.
  • Astronauts on the International Space Station have actually played Roblox while orbiting Earth.
  • Before Robux became the sole currency, the game had a secondary form of money called Tickets (or "Tix") that players earned just by logging in daily. Tix were discontinued in 2016.
