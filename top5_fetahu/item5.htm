FUN FACTS

  • It blew up so fast at launch in August 2020 that its servers crashed immediately because the developers underestimated the traffic by millions of players.
  • The game was pitched and developed under the longer title Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout before it was shortened to just Fall Guys.
  • According to the official developers, the cute little bean characters are actually 6 feet (1.83 meters) tall and have a bizarre, bird-like skeletal structure.
